# Seven predeclared illustrative market probes

Protocol `evals/market-coverage-protocol.json` was committed as `ac5a454` before capture. It declared seven city/artist pools, three slots, a genre cap of two, illustrative fees and availability, one offered-only re-rank per case, and the first tool-free model response only. Research used real Qloo with deterministic research-budget allocation; these new cases do not claim a model-step trace. No pool, price or control was repaired in response to outcomes.

## Observed capture

All seven snapshots were retained and added to the published manifest. London, Amsterdam, Toronto, Los Angeles and São Paulo have complete offered-pool ranking and feasible first-trial LLM controls. Paris and Sydney have incomplete ranking; no model call was made for either. Paris also has no feasible baseline at its declared budget. It remains published with that explicit result; the budget was not relaxed to make it look successful.

The capture used **138 Qloo workflow attempts**, below the declared maximum of 175, and **five model requests**, below the declared maximum of seven. Workflow counts are not claims about underlying HTTP-request count or monetary cost. The conservative LLM reservation cap was 60,000 tokens; 30,774 were reserved and the provider reported 4,192 actually used. This is not an inferred monetary charge. Each successful control used the pinned Groq `openai/gpt-oss-120b` model without Qloo observations or tools. Each of these seven briefs has at most one model attempt, so this cohort does not estimate repeat-to-repeat model variance. The separate recorded-case study retains three responses on one brief.

The public suite now has **12 distinct saved planning briefs and artist pools across 11 cities**, with eight comparable saved first-trial controls. These counts meet the distinct-input coverage target only. They do not establish independently sampled organizer demand, professional usefulness, representative markets or commercial superiority. Artist fees, currencies, availability and program genre labels are deliberately illustrative assumptions. USD in Toronto, São Paulo and Sydney is the hypothetical contract currency, not an assertion about local ticket prices or contracts. No professional reviewer or observed ticket-sales outcome has been supplied.
