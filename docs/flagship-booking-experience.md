# Flagship AI booking desk

The public judge page now connects a saved brief, recorded Qloo research, a local signal laboratory, interactive booking constraints and a promoter decision report in one continuous workflow. It retains the existing optimizer, authentication boundaries, public source records and exact experiment endpoints.

## Product changes

- A sticky five-step journey connects the brief, AI research, cultural proof, booking challenge and final decision. Original art and organizer tools remain available.
- The research desk surfaces authentic rank, geography and audience-comparison receipts. Stage cards scroll horizontally; the full recorded event sequence remains inspectable. Receipt-only cases still disclose the missing agent-event log.
- Four accessible signal switches expose all 16 saved coalitions directly in the decision room, with lineup differences, feasibility, fees, receipt links and internal policy attribution. This lab is explicitly tied to the original brief. A changed simulator budget never changes its locked comparison inputs. No new provider request is made.
- The simulator adds a budget slider, 100/90/80/70 percent presets, cancellation cards and clear pending/result states. Inputs require an explicit calculation; dragging the slider does not spend request quota. Failed replays preserve and identify the last completed decision.
- The decision board shows selected artists, illustrative fees, unallocated artist-fee budget, feasible search count and evidence status. Feasible swaps come directly from the existing optimizer. The promoter text export includes the completed budget, constraints, limitations and proof findings; unsubmitted changes are explicitly flagged. Existing JSON audit export remains available.

## Evidence boundaries

All claims come from the published projection and existing experiment reports. No new research trace, reviewer judgment, artist quote, ticket demand or commercial improvement is invented. Policy attribution points explain the optimizer's objective and are not audience outcomes. The recorded case still has no matched first-trial LLM-only control. Its signal withdrawal is a fixed-pool internal diagnostic.

## Verification and release

Browser coverage exercises the unified signal lab, no-extra-research behavior, exact simulated output, promoter export, survivor preservation, explicit infeasibility, signal-load retry, and failed-replay pending state, alongside the earlier mobile, receipt, provenance and authentication regressions. Anonymous release checks require the new desk markup and served script. Financial job benchmarks still exercise complete 10,000/100,000-draw jobs and a 100,000-capacity no-break-even scenario.

Before/after screenshots and exact check logs are retained locally under `artifacts/booking-*`. Production is not changed by this development milestone. The specific verified commit needs approval before deployment; hosted checks must be rerun afterward.
