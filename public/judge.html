Researching your show with Qloo

    The job

    Every bill starts with a room to fill.

    Houselights does not guess who will come. It asks what this city’s fans already love, then books what the budget allows.

    Qloo knows what your city loves. Houselights turns it into a bill you can actually book, and shows its working.

    How it works

    Four steps, all of them visible.

    1. Ask Qloo who your audience lovesQloo’s taste graph finds artists linked to your anchor artist’s fans in your city.
    2. Rank them for this crowdQloo scores every candidate for the same audience and city, and checks where the fans are.
    3. Check every possible billAn exact optimizer tries every combination and books the best one that fits your budget and rules.
    4. Show the receiptsEvery claim links to the saved Qloo response. Turn Qloo off to see what the same rules would book.

    Questions

    What judges usually ask.

    Are the fees real quotes?

    No. Saved plans use illustrative fees, and new acts get an estimate from their Qloo popularity tier. Every fee is labelled as such. Qloo has no booking fees, so nothing here is a quote.

    What does Qloo actually decide?

    Qloo supplies the taste evidence: which artists the anchor’s fans love in that city, how strongly, and where those fans are. The optimizer then picks the bill. Turn Qloo off and the same rules book a different bill in the featured New York case, in 200 of 200 fee variations we tested.

    Will this sell more tickets?

    We do not know yet. Qloo affinity measures cultural fit, not ticket demand, and no ticketing outcomes have been collected. The evidence page lists every claim with its limits.

    Can it break my budget or rules?

    No. The optimizer checks every possible bill and only books one that satisfies the budget and every rule. If none does, it says so instead of bending a rule.

    How many Qloo calls does a plan make?

    At most 12, with a two minute deadline. The progress panel shows each call as it happens, and each one becomes a receipt you can open.

    Where is the full methodology?

    In the full Booking Desk lab, with Autopilot, the original research timeline and the method comparisons, and on the evidence page.
