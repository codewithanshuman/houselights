Researching your show with Qloo

    How Houselights decides

    1. Ask Qloo who your audience loves. Qloo’s taste graph finds artists linked to your anchor artist’s fans in your city.
    2. Rank them for this crowd. Qloo scores every candidate against the same audience and city, then checks where fans are and which acts are trending.
    3. Check every possible bill. An exact optimizer tries every combination and books the best one that fits your budget and rules. It never guesses and never breaks a rule.
    4. Show the receipts. Every claim links to the saved Qloo response behind it. Turn Qloo off to see what the same rules would book without it.

    Qloo measures cultural affinity, not ticket sales. Fees on saved plans are illustrative and fees for new acts are estimates from Qloo popularity. Nothing is booked. Evidence and limits · Full Booking Desk lab
