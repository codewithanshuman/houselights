What do the terms on this page mean?
Qloo affinity
Qloo's score for how strongly an artist's audience overlaps in taste with the reference artists in this city. It measures cultural fit, not ticket sales.
Near-tie
Two artists whose affinity scores differ by less than 0.002, a threshold Houselights sets (Qloo publishes no margin of error). The gap is treated as too small to decide on, so the choice is yours.
Policy points
The optimizer's internal score: affinity plus small bonuses for local evidence, trends and variety, minus a penalty for missing evidence. A ranking tool, not a forecast.
Feasible
The bill fits the budget, the slot count, availability and every rule you set.
Not comparable
Qloo did not rank every candidate, so artists cannot be compared on fit. Those briefs are excluded from comparisons.
Evidence withdrawal
Re-running the same decision with Qloo's data removed, to see whether Qloo actually changed the choice.
Stress test
Re-planning under cancellations, fee rises and budget cuts to see whether the plan survives.
Receipt
A saved record of one Qloo request and what it returned, so every claim can be checked.
Decision proof
A recalculation record: the same inputs re-run to show how the choice was reached and what would change it. It shows the reasoning is consistent, not that the bill will sell more tickets.
